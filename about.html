About Us

A textile house, built around the cloth.

Asa Fabric is a textile house supplying quality fabrics — cottons, linens and more — to businesses worldwide, by the roll or the cut. We keep a working stockroom of cloth chosen for hand-feel, shade and everyday reliability, and organise it by use so buyers reach the right material quickly.

Our focus is straightforward: careful sourcing, honest stock information, and service that respects a production schedule. Whether you need a full roll or just the metres for a run, we aim to confirm quickly and dispatch cleanly.

We work with makers, ateliers and production teams across markets, matching each to the lots that suit their project. If we do not hold exactly what you are after, we will say so — and help you find the closest fit.
